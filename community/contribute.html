Get Involved

Help Build the Library

DesignersHub is a community-driven project. Your expertise can help thousands of beginners find their way.

Why Contribute?

By sharing your articles, roadmaps, or cheatsheets, you build your personal brand, help others, and contribute to the collective design knowledge base.

How to Submit Content

1. Write your Article

We prefer Markdown format, but we also accept Google Docs. Focus on actionable insights and clear examples.

2. Open a Pull Request

If you're technical, fork our repo on GitHub and submit a PR for your article file in the /blog or /ui-ux silos.

3. Review Process

Our editorial team (you!) will review the content for quality and formatting before it goes live.

Submission Methods

  • GitHub: [github.com/DesignersHub](https://github.com)
  • Email: [contribute@designershub.com](mailto:contribute@designershub.com)
  • Form: [Fill out our Submission Form](https://google.com/forms)
