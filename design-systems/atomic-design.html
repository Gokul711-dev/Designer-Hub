Atomic Design: Building from Atoms to Pages

Master the methodology for creating scalable and maintainable design systems, popularized by Brad Frost.

The Chemistry of UI

In Atomic Design, we break down our interface into five distinct levels:

  1. Atoms: The smallest building blocks (Input fields, buttons, labels).
  2. Molecules: Groups of atoms functioning together (A search bar made of a label, input, and button).
  3. Organisms: Complex components composed of molecules and/or atoms (A navigation header).
  4. Templates: Page-level objects that place organisms into a layout.
  5. Pages: Specific instances of templates with real content.

Why It Works

Atomic design allows you to transition between abstract and concrete. You can see how a single button (Atom) affects the entire design system at the page level.

Implementation in Figma

Use Components for your Atoms and Molecules. Assemble them into Nested Components for Organisms. This ensures that a change at the Atom level ripples through your entire file instantly.

Pro Handoff Tip

Developers love Atomic Design because it mirrors the structure of modern front-end frameworks like React and Vue, where everything is a component.
