Psychology Guide

E-commerce & Conversion Psychology

Master the art of design that sells. Learn the patterns that drive user behavior on product pages.

The F-Pattern and Z-Pattern

Users don't read; they scan. For text-heavy sites, users follow an F-Pattern. For conversion-focused landing pages, the Z-Pattern is more effective—leading the eye from the logo to the CTA.

Visual Hierarchy

Your primary Call to Action (CTA) must be the most visually distinct element. Use high contrast and whitespace to make the "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now" button unavoidable.

Friction & Flow

Every field in a checkout form is a potential drop-off point. Reduce friction by using address autocomplete, guest checkouts, and progress indicators for multi-step forms.

Pro Strategy Tip

Use "Social Proof" (reviews, testimonials) and "Trust Signals" (secure badges, clear return policies) near the final purchase button to reduce buyer's anxiety.
