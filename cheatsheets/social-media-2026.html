Reference Guide

Social Media Sizes 2026

The most up-to-date dimensions for every major social platform. Save this as your master reference.

Instagram

  • Profile Picture: 320 x 320 px
  • Post (Square): 1080 x 1080 px
  • Post (Portrait): 1080 x 1350 px
  • Stories & Reels: 1080 x 1920 px

LinkedIn

  • Profile Picture: 400 x 400 px
  • Cover Image: 1584 x 396 px
  • Post Image: 1200 x 1200 px (Square)

Twitter (X)

  • Profile Picture: 400 x 400 px
  • Header Image: 1500 x 500 px
  • Post Image: 1200 x 675 px (16:9)

Pro Asset Tip

Always design in high resolution (Double the size) to ensure your assets look crisp on Retina and high-density mobile displays.
