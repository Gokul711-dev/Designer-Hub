Figma vs Penpot: The Battle for the Web

Industry standard vs. Open-source heavyweight. Which one should your team choose?

Figma (The Goliath)

Figma is the industry standard for a reason. Its ecosystem of plugins, advanced prototyping (Variables, Logic), and sheer speed of adoption make it the safe choice for most enterprise teams.

Penpot (The Open Challenger)

Penpot is the first Open Source design and prototyping platform meant for domain-level teams. Its secret weapon? It uses SVG and Flexbox/CSS Grid natively. This means what you design is much closer to how it will be built in code.

Why Open Source?

With Penpot, you own your files. There is no vendor lock-in, and you can self-host the entire platform for maximum security and privacy.

Pro Decision Tip

If you're at a startup with a limited budget and a heavy focus on technical handoff, choose Penpot. If you're looking for a job at a large agency, master Figma.
