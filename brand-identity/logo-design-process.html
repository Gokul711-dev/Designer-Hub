The Logo Design Process

From the first sketch to the final vector export. Learn the technical workflow of a professional brand designer.

1. Discovery & Mind Mapping

Before you draw, you must think. Start with the "Luis Llamas" strategy: Define the constraints. Who is the target audience? What is the core emotion of the brand? Create a mind map of keywords and visual metaphors.

2. Sketching (The Analog Filter)

Never start in digital tools. Use paper to explore 50+ rough concepts in 30 minutes. This prevents you from getting attached to a single idea too early.

Iterate Quickly

Most of your first 20 ideas will be clichés. You have to burn through the obvious to reach the unique concepts.

3. Vectorization & Geometry

Bring your best sketches into Illustrator or Inkscape. Use geometric shapes and the Golden Ratio where appropriate to create a sense of balance. Ensure the logo works in pure black and white first.

Pro Vector Tip

Always use the minimum number of anchor points possible. Clean paths results in smaller file sizes and better scalability for print.
