Creating Brand Books & Guidelines

How to document a brand identity so it remains consistent across every touchpoint.

What is a Brand Book?

A Brand Book (or Style Guide) is the "Manual of Operations" for a brand's visual and verbal identity. It ensures that any designer or marketer working on the brand follows the same rules.

Core Sections

  • Mission & Values: The "Why" behind the brand.
  • Logo Usage: Spacing (Clearance), sizing, and what NOT to do.
  • Color Palette: Primary and secondary colors (HEX, RGB, CMYK).
  • Typography: Approved font families and hierarchy.
  • Voice & Tone: How the brand speaks to its audience.

Digital vs. PDF Guidelines

Modern brands use interactive digital guidelines (built in tools like Zeroheight or Notion). These allow developers to copy hex codes directly and designers to download assets from a single source of truth.

Pro Strategy Tip

Always include a "Logo Misuse" page. Show exactly how the logo should NOT be stretched, recolored, or placed on busy backgrounds to maintain brand integrity.
